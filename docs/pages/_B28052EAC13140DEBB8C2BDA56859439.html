System Set-up 

Localiser set-up

In the next section of the KUBE help TOC, you can find information about setting up your localiser. Generally speaking, setting up the localiser is a three steps process:

  • Localiser Configuration: When the hardware assembly is complete the next step is to start a specific utility software to configure the localiser.

  • Localiser Probe Calibration: Calibrating a localiser is needed to maintain a high degree of accuracy.

  • Localiser Performance Check: This section describes how to check if the localiser performance is within specification.

Alignment

  • Process: Alignment is the process of registering the sensor co-ordinate system to the localiser co-ordinate system. Without an accurate alignment, the data produced by the ModelMaker system will be out of specification.

  • Alignment Check: This section describes three methods of checking accuracy and repeatability of the ModelMaker system.
