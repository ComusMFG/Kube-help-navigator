Preferences - Display - Localiser Position

Localiser Position settings are used for formatting the co-ordinates of the Localiser Position window:

Digits format group

Left of decimal

Number of digits (including leading zeros if enabled) left of decimal point. Significant digits are not limited by this parameter.

Right of decimal

Number of digits right of decimal point.

Display leading zeros

Sets whether to display or not leading zeros if the number of significant digits is less than the number set for 'Left of decimal' parameter.

Always display sign

Sets whether to display or not the plus sign.

Preview

Shows the representation of the numbers according to the settings in this group.
