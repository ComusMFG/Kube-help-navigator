X System - Close-Up

Each picture indicates a cabling connection between one of the components of the system.

MMIB-D connections

 

The sensor cable transfers captured data, whereas the remote control antenna is used to receive signal from remote switches.

 

There are three cables connected to the back of the MMIB-D: the trigger cable, the switches and the SURFA PCI2 cable.

PC connections

 

The SURFA PCI2 cable connects the MMIB-D to the PCI2 board in the PC. 

 

Localiser serial cableprovides the link to the localiser power adaptor.

 

Dongle controls software options. 

Sensor connection

 

The sensor cable transfers data from the sensor to the MMIB-D.

Arm connections

 

The trigger cable connects the MMIB-D to the localiser's controller box.

 

Power cable of the FARO localiser.

 

The localiser power adaptor is connected to the PC via a serial cable and the power cable connects it to the arm.
