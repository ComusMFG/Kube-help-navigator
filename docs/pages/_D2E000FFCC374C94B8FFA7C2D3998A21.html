MMIB

Describes some of the problems related to ModelMaker Interface Box.

Wrong MMIB

KUBE only supports MMIB-D, MMIB-D2, and MMIB-DL. If you are trying to use any other hardware, an error message will appear advising to upgrade your equipment to the appropriate one.

 

Hardware malfunctioning

If this message appears during startup, this indicates communication problems between the SURFA PCI and MMIB. The SURFA PCI, MMIB, and/or cable may be faulty.

  • Check your hardware first.

  • Try restarting KUBE and also try pressing 'Refresh MMIB / Sensor status' button in System > Hardware Settings > Calibration and Alignment.

  • Ensure the KUBE drivers are installed correctly.
