Pick Pair of Points

This command is accessible from Measure > Pick Pairs of Points or from Pick Pairs of Points button on the toolbar. If Pick Pairs of Points is not selected on the toolbar, hold down the left mouse button on the current selection for a second to show up the drop-down toolbar. Select Pick Pairs of Points selection button from there.

Description: The function Pick Pair of Points is used to measure the on-screen distances between points.

Select Point 1 by left-clicking the mouse on the desired point. Information about Point 1 will appear in the information box. Then select Point 2. Information about Point 2 and the differences between Point 1 and Point 2 will be displayed in the box.

   Mouse pointers in Pick Pairs Of Points mode

  • Absolute Difference distance between Point 1 and Point 2 on the actual object.

  • Point one X, Y, and Z co-ordinates of Point 1.

  • Point two X, Y, and Z co-ordinates of Point 2.

  • Difference distance between Point 1 and Point 2 in terms of the X, Y and Z planes.

This information will also be copied in Output pane.

Example

  • Screen Difference distance between Point 1 and Point 2 in terms of the screen difference. This value will change depending on the 'Zoom' level on the object.

 

Exit Pick Pair of Points mode by clicking on the Close button on the tab.
