Tree

Every item in the entity tree has a checkbox to the left side of its icon. This interface element is used to control the hiding abilities.

Visualisation

Click on a checkbox to hide/show the corresponding entity or whole group. Hidden entities and groups, which all entities are hidden, are grayed out and their checkboxes are unchecked. If you click on the root, you will hide/show the whole scene.

 

Selection

Click on an icon to select/unselect the corresponding entity or whole group. Selected entities and groups with selected entities are highlighted in red. If you click on the root, you will select/unselect the whole scene, no matter whether there are hidden entities on not.

 

Lock/Unlock

Right-click on an icon or group of icons and from the context menu select Lock in Space/Unlock to lock or unlock the corresponding entity/entities or whole group. Locked entities and groups are marked with a push-pin.
