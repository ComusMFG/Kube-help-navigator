Sensor Video Connection Failed

A connection to the sensor has failed due to video connections.

 

No Video error message

The error message appears when the software is unable to detect a video signal from the sensor. This may be due to the following reasons:

  • The sensor video and/or power lead is not connected or faulty - ensure that the cables between the MMIB, SURFA PCI, and sensor are securely connected.

  • A fuse may have blown - check the LEDs on the front of the MMIB.
