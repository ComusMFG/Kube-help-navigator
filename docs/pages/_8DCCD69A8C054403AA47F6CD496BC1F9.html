CimCore Localiser Set-up

Installing a CimCore localiser

CimCore localiser installation consists of hardware set-up and configuration of WinRDS utility software.

 

Handling your localiser

The localiser is a precision measuring instrument, please treat it with care.

The localiser has also been designed to withstand a typical shop-floor environment, nevertheless, please ensure that it is handled with care according to the manufacturer's instructions.

 

CimCore localiser firmware

KUBE will only scan if the correct WinRDS is being used and firmware versions installed in the 3000i or INFINITE localiser.

Please use:
WinRDS v5.0.3 and encoders firmware Run Code 178.

To check the firmware version: Double click on the 'Localiser Utilities' shortcut icon on desktop and follow the procedure step by step as shown below:

  1. Click on the 'About Arm Utilities' button.

  2. A new window will appear called 'Arm Diagnosis'. Check the firmware version as displayed:

For instructions on how to upgrade firmware, please contact the localiser distributor.

CimCore localiser set-up process

CimCore 3000i:

  • Ensure the localiser is rigidly mechanically mounted (as per CimCore manual).

  • Ensure WinRDS is installed according to the manufacturers instructions.

  • Ensure that all the power switches on the CimCore 3000i components are turned off before conducting assembly.

  • Connect all the cables:

    Align the red dots of the serial cable's end and the connector on the back panel of the arm. Gently push the serial cable into the socket until a firm connection is established.

    Connect the 3 PIN male cable to the female adapter from power supply.
     

  • Connect the serial cable to the PC's serial port.

  • Connect the AC power cord to the power supply.

CimCore INFINITE:

  • Ensure the localiser is rigidly mechanically mounted (as per CimCore manual).

  • Ensure WinRDS is installed according to the manufacturers instructions.

  • Ensure that all the power switches on the CimCore INFINTE components are turned off before conducting assembly.

  • Connect all the cables:

Plug the USB cable on the back panel of the arm and gently tighten up the white screw. Connect its second end to the laptop.

Connect the trigger cable to the scanner lead on the back panel and to a network outlet of the laptop.

  • Connect the AC power cord to the power supply.

Starting the CimCore 3000i/INFINITE

  • Before powering up your CimCore 3000i/INFINITE system, you should check the AC outlet that it to be used.

If an outlet is used that does not produce the "Correct" code on the circuit tester, there us a  risk of damaging all or part of the CimCore 3000i/INFINITE system.

  • After checking the outlet, plug in the PC's power cord and the power cord that goes with the power supply.

  • Check all connections.

  • Power up the computer unit.

  • Power up the CimCore 3000i/INFINITE localiser. It's switch is located on the back panel. The localiser power switch will illuminate a green light and the localiser will also beep once to indicate that it is receiving power.

Confirming communications

  • Ensure that the WinRDS software key is properly placed on the printer parallel port.

  • Power up the complete system.

  • Connect the 15mm ball probe to the localiser by aligning the pins at the base of the probe with the sockets and the guide pins with the guide slots.

  		•

     

    Correct alignment is assisted by matching the alignment hole on the localiser's socket with the slightly recessed hole on the shaft of the probe.
     

  • Insert the probe straight and lock it snugly in place by twisting the probe adapter (black twisting handle).

Do not force the probe. Do not over-tighten probe adapter, but ensure it is well attached.

  • Double click on the 'Arm Utilities' shortcut icon on desktop screen. WinRDS will begin to communicate with the localiser.

  • Place the localiser in the 'home' position to reset the axes.

The 'home' position is obtained by placing the A, B and C axes (shoulder to elbow) parallel to the surface and D, E and F axes (elbow to finger) perpendicular to the surface.

Rotate the localiser by moving all the axes. As each axes is set, the bars will turn blue.

  •  

  • Once all the bars have turned blue, the localiser now acts as a 3 dimensional mouse for screen selections while in WinRDS. The movement of the wrist and finger (E and F axes) allows fluid mouse control. The localiser's middle button acts as the left mouse button.

  • Verify the WinRDS ArmSpecs conversion by lacing the probe in a hole that cups the 15mm ball probe while moving the elbow axis and watching the movement of the Z value on the screen.

There should be very little variation if the data was correctly translated.

  • To exit WinRDS, place the screen pointer on the box with the x in the upper right corner of the screen (Windows close button) and press the localiser's middle button.

Note: ArmSpecs data is located in a folder ARMDATA under C:\Program Files\CimCore\WinRDS\. This folder also contains each of the probe profiles.
