Temporary circle

When all values are set the new circle will be visualised in the viewport as temporary entity. There are different types of temporary circles:

  • temporary construction circle - when a circle takes part in other entity definition, it appears in the viewport as temporary construction datum (white/black coloured by default). Its appearance can be changed from System > Preferences > Display > Colours > Temporary Construction Datum

  • current construction circle - the currently edited/selected circle appears in the viewport as highlighted temporary datum (light red by default). Its colour can be changed from System > Preferences > Display > Colours > Highlighted.

Notes: If the temporary construction datum is currently edited, it appears as highlighted temporary datum.

  • final construction circle - when all definition fields/all construction tabs are filled, then the circle appears in the viewport as final construction datum (green/black coloured by default). Its appearance can be changed from System > Preferences > Display > Colours > Final Construction Datum.

Notes: If the current construction datum is actually the final one, then it will be coloured as final construction datum.

When this final construction datum is really created then its colour will be changed to the real datum colour (set in System > Preferences > Display > Colours ).

The normal direction of the circle is indicated by an arrow and by the movement of the points on its dashed line.
