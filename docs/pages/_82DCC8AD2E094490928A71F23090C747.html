Introduction

The use of 3D models in simulation and design for industrial processes, automotive industries, engineering process, entertainment and computer games has increased dramatically during the past years.

With its intuitive interface and no need for previous 3D scanning or data processing experience, KUBE is the perfect tool for both professionals and beginners requiring precision in their work.

 

This Help file has been designed for use as a guide or manual for KUBE users. It is intended to supply immediate support in response to user queries and to assist the user in improving effectiveness and efficiency while working with KUBE. Rich, illustrated, and detailed, it will be your assistant in 3D scanning and post-processing.
